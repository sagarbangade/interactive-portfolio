STEP 03 — MASTER PROMPT
 MASTER PROMPT — PART 1
master prompt
I have placed a character animation video in `public/character.mp4`. The video features a character
against a solid background whose head turns in 8 directional poses (clockwise/counter-clockwise),
with a neutral pose looking directly at the camera at the end.
I want you to build an award-winning, luxury portfolio hero section with an ultra-smooth, zero-lag,
zero-ghosting cursor-tracking character animation.
### CRITICAL TECHNICAL CONSTRAINTS (DO NOT VIOLATE):
1. **DO NOT USE CSS 3D TRANSFORMS ON THE PAGE/VIDEO:** Never use `perspective`, `rotateX`, or
`rotateY` on the canvas or container. The page and character's body must stay 100% rock-solid
motionless. ONLY the character's head should rotate.
2. **DO NOT SEEK OR PLAY THE MP4 VIDEO AT RUNTIME IN THE BROWSER:** Generated MP4s have only 1
keyframe. In-browser `video.currentTime` seeking causes severe lag and freezes, and calling
`video.play()` causes uncontrolled background playback.
3. **PRE-EXTRACT WEBP FRAMES WITH PYTHON / OPENCV:**
  - Write a python script using `cv2` to inspect the video's frame count and timeline.
  - Identify the exact frame numbers for the 8 compass directions (UP, UP-RIGHT, RIGHT, DOWN-RIGHT,
DOWN, DOWN-LEFT, LEFT, UP-LEFT) and the CENTER neutral frame.
  - Pre-extract 64 high-quality WebP frames along the 360° circular head rotation trajectory into
`public/frames/` (each frame ~5.6° apart), plus `center.webp`.
4. **ZERO-GHOSTING 60 FPS CANVAS RENDERER:**
  - Preload the frames in React into an image array.
  - In a `requestAnimationFrame` loop, calculate the cursor's angle relative to the character's face
center (`atan2(dy, dx)`).
  - Use shortest-path circular angular lerp (`lerpAngle`) with a fast response factor (`~0.26`) so
it tracks in ~35ms with zero lag.
  - Map the smoothed angle to the nearest frame index (0..63).
  - Draw **EXACTLY ONE crisp frame at 100% opacity** on the canvas. DO NOT alpha-blend overlapping
frames (alpha blending creates double-face ghosting).
5. **CENTER EYE CONTACT (DEADZONE):**
  - When the cursor is near the character's face (within ~12% screen radius), switch to
`center.webp` so she looks directly into the user's eyes.
6. **SEAMLESS BACKGROUND:**
  - Detect the exact RGB hex color of the video background and match the page and canvas background
to it seamlessly.
